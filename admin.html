    Promotions

    Manage offers shown on the Promotions page and accepted at checkout. Dates use Philippine time. Delivery offers still require an address within your delivery area.

    Approved resort partners

    Ask each partner to register on the Account page and verify their email first. Approve that email below. Partners must sign in with that account to use RESORT50 on every qualifying order.

    To change the qualifying amount, open Resort partner intro below, edit Minimum order (PHP), then Save promotions. Deactivation blocks future uses; existing orders keep their prices.

    Automatic free delivery needs no code. Change its amount under Settings → Delivery & fees → Automatic free delivery minimum. The Promotions page displays that saved amount.

    Tasting box requests

    Review business requests and contact the business to arrange a tasting box. Approval here does not create an order or approve a resort partner account.

    Email notifications use Settings → New-order and tasting-request email alerts. Requests remain here if email is disabled or fails.
